About Space Design

Independent by design.

A project management consultancy for housing-society redevelopment, with offices in Vashi, Navi Mumbai and Shivaji Nagar, Pune.

Who we are

30 years of redevelopment, always on the society’s side

Led by Ar. Milind Fulzele, Space Design has completed 125+ redevelopment projects and served 50+ clients across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. The firm stays completely independent of developers, so every recommendation is made in the society’s interest.

Societies rely on us for transparent process, detailed feasibility reports and timely execution, and for smart, efficient and affordable space planning shaped by MHADA redevelopment.
