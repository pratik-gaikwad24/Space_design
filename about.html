About Space Design

30 years of experience. Independent by design.

Redevelopment PMC · Architects · Planners

Space Design is a project management consultancy specialising in housing-society redevelopment, with its head office at Satra Plaza, Vashi, Navi Mumbai and a branch office in Shivaji Nagar, Pune.

01

About Space Design

With over 30 years of experience, Space Design has completed 125+ redevelopment projects and served 1,000+ clients, with several more projects ongoing across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.

The firm is known for transparency, technical expertise and timely execution. It provides detailed feasibility reports that help housing societies assess their redevelopment potential, and it remains completely independent of developers, so that decisions stay unbiased and focused on the society.

Led by Ar. Milind Fulzele, the firm is also recognised for smart, efficient and affordable space planning, with a design approach shaped by MHADA redevelopment.

Questions & answers

01

What does Space Design do?

Space Design is a project management consultancy specialising in housing-society redevelopment. Its team of architects, planners and engineers also provides architecture, MHADA layout planning and interior design. See services.

02

Is Space Design independent of developers?

Yes. Space Design works for the housing society and remains independent of developers, so that decisions are unbiased and focused on the society.

03

Which MHADA boards is Space Design empanelled with?

Space Design is empanelled at Grade 1 with the MHADA Mumbai, Pune, Konkan and Amravati boards, is MHADA layout architect for several layouts, and is PMC for self-redevelopment with the Mumbai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd.

04

Who leads Space Design?

Space Design is led by its founder and sole proprietor, Ar. Milind Fulzele, B.Arch (Hons), A.I.I.A., a graduate of Sir J.J. College of Architecture, University of Mumbai, with 30 years of experience.
