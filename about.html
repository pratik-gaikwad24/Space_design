About Space Design

30 years of experience. A continuing approach to design.

Architects · Planners · Interior Designers · PMC

Space Design is a practice of architects, planners and interior designers, and a project management consultant, working from offices in Navi Mumbai and Pune.

01

About Space Design

With 30 years of experience in architecture, planning, interior design and project management consultancy, Space Design develops thoughtful spaces shaped by context, functionality and design.

The studio works across four connected disciplines, from the planning of a site to the design of a building, the detailing of its interiors and the management of its delivery. The head office is in Vashi, Navi Mumbai, with a branch office in Shivaji Nagar, Pune.

02

Our philosophy

Spaces should respond to where they are, work well for the people who use them, and be carefully made.

Context, function and design are considered together, rather than one at a time. The result is architecture and interiors that are clear in intent and practical in use.
