About Space Design

30 years of designing spaces for housing societies

Led by Ar. Milind Fulzele, Space Design is a project management consultancy specialising in housing-society redevelopment. It works independently of developers, so that every decision is made in the society’s interest, and provides detailed feasibility reports to help societies assess their redevelopment potential.
