04

How we work

Seven stages of redevelopment, from the society’s first decision to handover, with Space Design working for the society and independently of developers.

  1. 01

    Society appointment

    The society appoints Space Design as PMC for redevelopment guidance.

  2. 02

    Property study & feasibility

    Land potential, FSI, applicable rules and project feasibility are assessed.

  3. 03

    Member consultation

    Benefits and requirements are explained to members, and their inputs collected.

  4. 04

    Tender & developer selection

    Offers are invited and compared, and the society is helped to select the right developer.

  5. 05

    Agreement & approvals

    Support with legal documentation, planning and government approvals.

  6. 06

    Project monitoring

    Quality, timelines, construction progress and transparency are supervised.

  7. 07

    Handover & completion

    Proper possession, amenities and completion are ensured for every member.

  8. Next step

    Planning a redevelopment?
