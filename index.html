04

Design approach

Four considerations that run through every project, from a single interior to a site plan.

  1. Every project begins with its setting: the site, climate, surroundings and the people who will use it.

  2. Spaces are planned around how they will be used: movement, relationships between areas, light and practical requirements.

  3. Form develops from context and function, giving each building or interior a clear and coherent identity.

  4. Materials, junctions and finishes are resolved with care, so the design holds together from the overall plan to the final detail.
