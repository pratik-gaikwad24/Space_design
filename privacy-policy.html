Who we are

For the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and applicable rules, Space Design is the Data Fiduciary for the data described here.

Scope

This notice covers this website only: the public pages, the enquiry form and the private administration area used by authorised staff. It does not cover services provided under a separate engagement agreement, which may include their own data terms.

Personal data we collect

Enquiry form

When you submit the contact form we collect only what we need to reply:

  • Required: name, email address, phone number, message.
  • Optional: project type and project location.
  • Consent record: the fact that you ticked the Privacy Notice checkbox, the wording you agreed to, and the date and time of submission.

Please do not include sensitive information (for example financial, health or identity-document details) in your message.

Security and anti-spam data

To stop automated abuse, the form uses a hidden “honeypot” field and a minimum fill time in your browser. On our database, a one-way cryptographic hash of your IP address is kept for up to 2 days to limit repeated submissions. The raw IP address is not stored by us. Our hosting provider and Supabase may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.

Browsing the website

Visitors browsing the public pages are not asked to sign in, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. See the Cookie Policy.

Administrator accounts

For authorised staff only: email address, authentication records and actions performed in the admin area, processed by Supabase Auth.

Why we use it

  • To read and respond to your enquiry and discuss the project you asked about.
  • To keep a record of enquiries and of your consent.
  • To protect the website and form from spam, abuse and security incidents.

We do not use enquiry data for marketing newsletters, sell it, or share it with advertisers. If we ever wish to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

Consent and withdrawal

We process enquiry data on the basis of the consent you give by ticking the checkbox on the contact form. The checkbox is never pre-ticked.

You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing spacedesign1108@gmail.com with the subject “Withdraw consent”. We will stop processing and delete your enquiry data, unless we must retain it to comply with law. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to [CLIENT TO PROVIDE] retention period, e.g. 12 months after our last communication with you, unless the enquiry leads to an engagement, in which case records are kept as required for that engagement and by law. Enquiries past the retention period are flagged in the admin area and deleted.
  • Rate-limit hashes: automatically deleted after 2 days.
  • Technical logs: according to the retention settings of Supabase and the hosting provider.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Enquiries can be read only by authorised administrators. This is enforced in the database with Row Level Security, not only in the browser.
  • There is no public account registration. Admin accounts are created individually.
  • Secret keys are never included in the website’s code.
  • Uploaded files are validated by type and size.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act in accordance with applicable law, including notifying affected individuals and authorities where required.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the DPDP Act, you may:

  • request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • request correction, completion or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request erasure of your data where it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw consent (see above);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and, if not resolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise a right, email spacedesign1108@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within [CLIENT TO PROVIDE] response time, e.g. 30 days.
