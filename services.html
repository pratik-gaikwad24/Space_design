Service 01

Redevelopment

Independent project management consultancy for housing societies, from the first feasibility study to handover.

As PMC, Space Design works for the society, not for developers. It checks land potential, FSI and the applicable rules, explains the benefits and requirements to members, runs the tender and helps the society select the right developer, supports agreements and approvals, and monitors quality, timelines and progress until every member receives proper possession.

Discuss your redevelopment

How it works

  1. Society appointment

    Society appoints PMC for redevelopment guidance.

  2. Property study & feasibility

    Land potential, FSI, rules and project feasibility.

  3. Member consultation

    Benefits and requirements explained; member inputs collected.

  4. Tender & developer selection

    Offers invited, proposals compared, developer selected.

  5. Agreement & approvals

    Legal documentation, planning and government approvals.

  6. Project monitoring

    Quality, timelines, construction progress and transparency.

  7. Handover & completion

    Possession, amenities and completion for society members.

Service 02

Self-Redevelopment

Project management consultancy for housing societies that redevelop their own building, without handing it to a developer.

In self-redevelopment the society itself takes on the project, so the benefits of redevelopment stay with its members. Space Design guides the society through feasibility, finance, approvals, contractor appointment and construction, and keeps every step transparent to members.

Space Design is PMC for self-redevelopment with the Mumbai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., and its project director has 25 years of experience in redevelopment and self-development projects.

Discuss self-redevelopment

How it works

  1. Feasibility

    Land potential, FSI, rules and project viability.

  2. Member consent

    Proposal explained to members and resolutions passed.

  3. Finance

    Project funding arranged with the bank.

  4. Approvals

    Design, planning and government approvals.

  5. Construction

    Contractor appointment and site monitoring.

  6. Handover

    Possession and completion for society members.

Service 04

Architecture

Architecture for redevelopment and new buildings, with smart, efficient and affordable space planning.

Service 05

Planning

MHADA layout planning and layout revisions for large housing schemes.

As MHADA layout architect, Space Design plans and revises layouts that organise housing, roads, amenities, schools, commercial plots and open space. Current work includes revisions of the Maharshi Nagar layout in Pune, the Titwala layout in Kalyan, and the Morivali and S.A. Patil Nagar layouts in Ambernath.

Discuss a planning project

How it works

  1. Study

    Existing layout, land areas and site conditions.

  2. Framework

    Residential, commercial, amenity and open-space areas.

  3. Layout

    Plots, roads and circulation.

  4. Documentation

    Area statements and drawings for approval.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Common questions about redevelopment and our services. Anything else, ask us directly.

01

What does a redevelopment PMC do?

A redevelopment project management consultant (PMC) works for the housing society. Space Design studies the property and its feasibility, consults members, runs the tender and developer selection, supports agreements and approvals, monitors construction, and ensures proper handover.

02

What services does Space Design provide?

Space Design provides redevelopment, self-redevelopment and development services for housing societies, along with architecture, MHADA layout planning and interior design.

03

What is a feasibility report for redevelopment?

A feasibility report assesses a property's redevelopment potential: land potential, FSI, the applicable rules and whether the project is viable, so that the society can make an informed decision.

04

Does Space Design work on self-redevelopment?

Yes. Space Design is PMC for self-redevelopment with the Mumbai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., and its project director has 25 years of experience in redevelopment and self-development projects.

05

What is interior design?

Interior design is the planning and design of the inside of a space: layout, furniture, lighting, materials, finishes and details, so that the space works well for the people who use it.
