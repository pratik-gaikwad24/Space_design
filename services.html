Architecture

Illustration · replace before launch

Architectural design shaped by context, functionality and the way people use space.

Architecture covers the design of a building from the first ideas to the drawings used to build it. The work considers the site and its surroundings, the brief and its requirements, and how the building will be used and experienced.

  1. Brief

  3. Development

    Resolving design, drawings and coordination.

  4. Delivery

    Supporting execution through to completion.

02

Planning

Illustration · replace before launch

Planning that organises sites, buildings and movement into clear, workable layouts.

Architectural planning organises a site or building: where built form sits, how people and vehicles move, how spaces relate to one another, and how the layout meets the requirements that apply to the project.

Discuss a planning project →

  1. Study

    Site analysis, context and applicable parameters.

  2. Framework

    Zoning, access, circulation and built-form strategy.

  3. Layout

    Detailed layouts and area planning.

  4. Documentation

    Drawings for review and approvals.

Interior Design

Illustration · replace before launch

Interiors that bring together space, light, material and detail.

Interior design is the planning and design of the inside of a space: its layout, furniture, lighting, materials, finishes and details. The aim is a space that works well and feels right for the people who use it.

Discuss an interior project →

  1. Brief

    Needs, use patterns and character of the space.

  2. Layout

    Space planning and furniture arrangement.

  3. Materials

    Finishes, lighting and detailing.

  4. Execution

    Coordination on site through completion.

Project Management Consultancy

Illustration · replace before launch

Consultancy that helps projects move from drawings to delivery with clarity and control.

Project management consultancy (PMC) supports a project through execution: planning scope and schedule, coordinating consultants, contractors and vendors, monitoring progress and quality on site, and managing handover.

  1. Planning
