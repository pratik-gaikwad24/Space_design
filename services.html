01

Redevelopment PMC

Independent project management consultancy for housing societies, from the first feasibility study to handover.

As PMC, Space Design works for the society, not for developers. It checks land potential, FSI and the applicable rules, explains the benefits and requirements to members, runs the tender and helps the society select the right developer, supports agreements and approvals, and monitors quality, timelines and progress until every member receives proper possession.

Space Design is also PMC for self-redevelopment with the Mumbai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd.

  3. Member consultation

    Benefits and requirements explained; member inputs collected.

  4. Tender & developer selection

    Offers invited, proposals compared, developer selected.

  5. Agreement & approvals

    Legal documentation, planning and government approvals.

  6. Project monitoring

    Quality, timelines, construction progress and transparency.

  7. Handover & completion

    Possession, amenities and completion for society members.

Planning

MHADA layout planning and layout revisions for large housing schemes.

As MHADA layout architect, Space Design plans and revises layouts that organise housing, roads, amenities, schools, commercial plots and open space. 05

Questions & answers

01

What does a redevelopment PMC do?

A redevelopment project management consultant (PMC) works for the housing society. Space Design studies the property and its feasibility, consults members, runs the tender and developer selection, supports agreements and approvals, monitors construction, and ensures proper handover.

02

What services does Space Design provide?

Space Design provides project management consultancy for housing-society redevelopment and self-redevelopment, architecture, MHADA layout planning and interior design.

03

What is a feasibility report for redevelopment?

A feasibility report assesses a property's redevelopment potential: land potential, FSI, the applicable rules and whether the project is viable, so that the society can make an informed decision.

04

Does Space Design work on self-redevelopment?

Yes. Space Design is PMC for self-redevelopment with the Mumbai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., and its project director has 25 years of experience in redevelopment and self-development projects.

05

What is interior design?

Interior design is the planning and design of the inside of a space: layout, furniture, lighting, materials, finishes and details, so that the space works well for the people who use it.
